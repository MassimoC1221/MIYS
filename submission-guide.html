AUTHOR GUIDANCE

Submission guide

MIYS aims to publish clear, honest and thoughtful mathematical writing. Work can be an original investigation, a proof of a known result written in the author's own words, or an expository article that helps readers understand a topic.

What we look for

  • Clarity: define notation and explain the question being explored.
  • Rigour: give complete arguments, state assumptions and check edge cases.
  • Honesty: distinguish known results from new observations; do not claim a famous open problem has been solved without exceptional verification.
  • Attribution: cite books, articles, websites and people whose ideas helped.
  • Accessibility: state the intended level and explain specialist terms where practical.

Suggested paper structure

  1. Title and author name or chosen byline.
  2. Abstract: a short summary of the question and main result.
  3. Introduction and background.
  4. Main definitions, lemmas, calculations or proof.
  5. Conclusion, limitations or questions for further work.
  6. References and acknowledgements.

Review and publication

In the founding phase, submissions are handled manually by the MIYS team. A submission may receive requests for clarification or correction before publication. Acceptance should not be described as formal peer review unless a defined and appropriately qualified review process has actually taken place.

Privacy and consent

Before publishing identifying details, obtain the author's informed permission. For contributors under 18, MIYS should establish a suitable safeguarding and consent process with a trusted adult before collecting personal information or publishing identifying details. Do not send sensitive personal information with a paper.

How to submit during the founding phase

For now, contact the founding team directly to discuss a submission. A secure online submission form will be added after the review process and privacy arrangements are in place.
