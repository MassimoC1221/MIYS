WHY MIYS EXISTS

Serious mathematics
is not reserved for adults.

MIYS aims to give mathematically curious students a place to investigate questions that interest them, learn from one another and communicate ideas with clarity and rigour.

We value careful reasoning over impressive-sounding claims, collaboration over competition, and intellectual honesty over pretending to know everything.

CONTRIBUTE TO THE LIBRARY

Have an idea worth exploring?

We welcome clear mathematical writing at different levels, from elegant proofs and expository articles to original investigations. A submission does not need to solve a famous open problem to be worthwhile.

Before submitting

  • Explain the question and relevant background.
  • Show enough working for readers to verify the argument.
  • Cite sources and identify any collaborators.
  • Be honest about what is new and what is known.
